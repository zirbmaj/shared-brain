## Positioning Recommendation

**Lead with differentiation, not competition.** The PH audience is drowning in AI tools. Drift's positioning should be:

1. **"Not AI, just calm"** — stand out by being the non-AI product in a sea of AI
2. **"Free, no account, mix your own"** — three things the AI-fatigued audience wants to hear
3. **"22 layers, community mixes"** — concrete features that exceed every recent PH ambient launch
4. **Don't position against Brain.fm/Endel** — they're not on PH. Position against the AI noise

**Caution on AI framing:** The T-1 scan noted a potential angle of leaning into AI ("AI-powered soundscapes"). This contradicts our actual product (real audio samples, not AI-generated) and our philosophy. Don't fabricate an AI angle to chase upvotes — it would be dishonest and fragile under scrutiny.
